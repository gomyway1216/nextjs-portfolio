# Anime blog covers

These 1200 × 630 WebP files are ready for the blog cover-image uploader. They form one editorial anime series around an original recurring protagonist: a friendly Japanese software engineer with short black hair, round black glasses, and a teal hoodie.

| File | Article concept |
| --- | --- |
| `settli-why-i-built.webp` | An izakaya bill becoming a small set of clean transfers |
| `srs-flashcards-engineering.webp` | Flashcards moving along increasingly spaced review intervals |
| `article-to-podcast.webp` | An article turning into a two-host conversation and waveform |
| `ai-daily-article-pipeline.webp` | A sunrise learning factory publishing a fresh daily article |
| `voicevox-nextjs-voice-chat.webp` | Japanese speech passing through an AI pipeline to an original voice mascot |

## Prompt set

All images use the built-in image generator and the `illustration-story` use case. They share these constraints: wide 1.91:1 composition, polished anime editorial illustration, clean cel shading, crisp linework, expressive faces, thumbnail-readable silhouettes, no title text, no readable labels, no logos, no watermarks, no photorealism, and no existing copyrighted characters.

### Settli

A cozy Japanese izakaya after dinner. The recurring protagonist and two friends gather around a phone while a messy pile of receipts and glowing coins resolves into three clear payment arrows. Warm lantern light; teal, mint, coral, amber gold, and deep navy.

### Spaced repetition

A magical study room opening into a memory galaxy. The protagonist flips a flashcard while cards launch from a messy stack and orbit outward at increasing intervals. Calendar symbols mark reviews, a tiny original quiz-bot presents surprise questions, and a brain constellation brightens. Raspberry, violet, teal, cyan, and gold.

### Article to podcast

A cozy futuristic recording studio blended with a train commute at dawn. The protagonist listens on headphones while an article flows into two distinct original radio-host avatars at microphones and becomes an audio waveform. Apricot orange and cool blue hosts; deep indigo, teal, cream, and cyan.

### Daily article pipeline

A whimsical automated learning workshop at sunrise. The protagonist arrives with coffee as an original writing-bot completes a fresh study article. A visual production line runs from a scheduler clock through varied topic cards into the article, then branches toward a phone, quiz, and audio waveform. Sunrise lavender, violet, teal, lemon, cream, and cyan.

### Japanese voice chat

A playful portfolio studio with a Tokyo-meets-Bay-Area night skyline. The protagonist speaks into a microphone; a glowing speech bubble passes through three cloud pipeline gates, and an original green bean-shaped voice mascot in a laptop answers with musical notes and a waveform. Aqua, mint, cyan, coral, violet-blue, and warm skin tones.
